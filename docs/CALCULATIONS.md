# Every calculation, step by step

Generated by `scripts/run_all.py` from the inputs in `data/`. Money is in GBP. “How many” always means *budget ÷ unit price*. Some cards then put that number in context.

## 1. The bill

- **Official total, 2024 brand refresh: £434,801.47.** Source: Imperial College London FOI disclosure log, 'Imperial Brand Project: FOI' (PDF created 26 Mar 2024) (https://www.imperial.ac.uk/media/imperial-college/administration-and-support-services/secretariat/public/information-governance/Imperial-Brand-Project-FOI.pdf). The total covers the external agencies (brand strategy; verbal and visual identity; design assets for launch; templating; training; accessibility and inclusivity assessment), a bespoke font, new signage across campuses, and promotional materials, including a brand animation video and lanyards.
- **No breakdown.** 'We cannot disclose the exact amount paid to each partner organisation/supplier' (exempt under s.43). No signage-only figure has been published. (https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/cost_of_re_design_for_new_imperi)
- **2002 rebrand: £298,300**, “spread over two years”. Source: Imperial FOI reply to A. Walshon, 24 Sep 2009 (WhatDoTheyKnow, attachment 'FOI branding.pdf') (https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/re_branding_of_imperial_college).

## 2. Today's money

ONS CPI index (D7BT, 2015 = 100): 2024 average = 133.9; 2002–03 average = 75.0; September 2026 = 144.1.

- 2024 refresh: £434,801.47 × 144.1 / 133.9 = **£467,923**
- 2002 rebrand: £298,300 × 144.1 / 75.0 = **£573,134**
- In real terms the 2002 rebrand cost 22% more than the 2024 refresh. Together: **£1,041,057**.
